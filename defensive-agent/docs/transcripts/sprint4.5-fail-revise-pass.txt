**Rule 1000000 (`/rest/user/login`) — final verdict: PASS** (7/7 replayed attacks blocked, 0/12 benign requests falsely blocked).

**Starting point:** `read_current_rule` returned an existing rule covering numeric `OR n=n` tautologies, quote/encoded-quote (`%27`, `&#x27;`) followed by a comment token, `;--`, `UNION ... SELECT`, `DROP/TRUNCATE TABLE`, `SLEEP(`, and `WAITFOR DELAY`. I treated all of that as a floor and carried every branch forward — nothing was dropped, even though today's sample only exercised a subset.

**Today's evidence (all one family, sqli — auth bypass on the login body):**
- `admin@juice-sh.op&#x27--` — HTML-entity quote without trailing `;`, then `--`
- ` OR 1=1%20--%20`, ` OR 1=1 %2F-- %2F` — URL-encoded whitespace/slash separators
- ` OR '1''='1 ` — quoted (non-numeric) tautology
- ` OR  1=1 `, ` OR 1=1;--`, ` or 1=1;-- ` — spacing/case/terminator variants

**What I added:** a quoted-string tautology branch (`OR '1''='1`, `or "a"="a"`), `AND` as well as `OR`, `+`/`%20` as whitespace, `%3d`/`%3b`/`%2d%2d` encodings, and `/` as a separator before a comment.

**False-positive correction:** the first attempt inherited and broadened the "quote followed by `--`, `#` or `;`" branch, which blocked three legitimate requests (`'apple'; juice`, `great 'service'; would buy again`, `'juice' #1 shop`). I narrowed it: a quote may now only trigger on `--`, or on `;` followed by a comment or a SQL keyword (`drop|select|update|delete|insert|union|truncate|exec`), and a bare `#` only at end-of-value. This slightly reduces coverage versus the original rule's blanket quote+`;`/`#` match, but that branch was demonstrably firing on ordinary punctuation — favoring benign traffic is the correct trade.

**Caveats:** two config attempts failed/were revised before passing — the first `test_waf_configuration` failure was caused by punctuation in the rule's `msg` text, not the regex. No non-attack entries appeared in `still_bypassing`. PASS means the rule covers the 7 recorded bypasses and the built-in benign set; obfuscations not present in this evidence (inline `/**/` comment splitting inside the `OR` clause, `||` concatenation, deeper double-encoding) are not guaranteed to be caught.
